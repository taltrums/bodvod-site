A workout planner that explains every pick

BodVod plans each strength workout from what you log: an estimate of what has recovered, and what your week still needs. It helps you log every set, and it all stays on your iPhone.

Planning

Today's workout, built from your own log

Open the app and the exercises, sets, reps and weights are ready, each with the reason it's there.

  • A plan for today

    Pick a focus such as Push, Legs or Upper, or leave it on Auto. BodVod chooses exercises for your goal and equipment and fits them to the time you have.

  • An estimate of what has recovered

    Every set adds fatigue to the muscles it works, and that fatigue fades over time. Tired muscles are steered away from, not banned.

  • A week that adds up

    Each muscle gets a minimum number of sets a week, shared across the days you train. Whatever is still owed comes first.

  • How you feel counts

    One optional question a day. Feel rough and you get fewer sets, each a rep further from failure. Mark a rest day and there's no plan at all.

  • Every number explained

    Tap ⓘ beside any number to see what it is, how it's worked out from your log, and what you can do about it.

  • Plan your week

    Set the days you usually train and change any single day on a calendar. Optional reminders ask "Training today?", suggest a meal two hours before, and give a heads-up 30 minutes before.

In the gym

Logging that keeps up with you

Made for quick taps between sets, wherever you train.

  • Last time, right there

    Each exercise shows what you did last time, like 80 kg × 8, 8, 7. Type the weight or nudge it with + and −.

  • Weights you can load

    Log reps and reps in reserve. Reach the top of the rep range with reps to spare and the weight goes up next time, only ever to a weight your bar, plates or dumbbells can make.

  • Rest, supersets and warm-ups

    A rest alert that works with the phone locked, supersets that take you straight to the partner exercise, and warm-up sets that build up to heavy lifts.

  • Gym, home, park or beach

    Choose a full gym, home gym, park, beach or bodyweight only, or list your own equipment. Training somewhere else today? Change it just for today.

  • Missing some equipment?

    Tap "Don't have the equipment?" on an exercise and BodVod swaps in the closest one your kit allows, for that workout only.

  • Form guides for 193 exercises

    Written cues for every exercise, pictures for most, and a short demo video for some. Videos load from YouTube only when you tap play.

Progress

Your progress, worked out from your log

Records, charts and recovery all come from what you logged, so fixing a set fixes everything that came from it.

  • Progress charts

    Estimated one-rep max on the lifts you do most, sets and workouts each week, and bodyweight, each with a table behind the chart.

  • Personal records

    Four kinds of record, spotted the moment you finish a set.

  • Fix any workout

    Correct a set, remove one that never happened, or log a past workout for the day you trained without your phone.

  • Share a workout

    Share an image or a story video in four looks, made on the phone. The video's numbers count up, and neither shows your bodyweight.

  • A character on Today

    Pick Kai, Mira, Ember or Pebble. Their rank, from E to S, follows how often you've trained on your planned days over the last four weeks. You can turn them off.

  • Apple Health, if you want it

    Turn it on and BodVod reads sleep, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, workouts and steps to help with your daily check-in and recovery estimate. Nothing is written back.

On-device AI

AI that stays on your iPhone

BodVod's suggestions are worked out on the phone, and each one comes with its reasoning.

PERSONALIZE THIS WORKOUT

Apple Intelligence picks between exercises

On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, tap Personalize this workout. Apple's on-device model can suggest up to three exercises from a shortlist the workout engine has already checked against your equipment and scored for your goal and recovery.

The engine sets the numbers

Sets, reps, weights, rest and time always come from BodVod's workout engine, which explains every pick. Without Apple Intelligence, the same engine plans every workout.

Nothing leaves the phone

The model runs on your iPhone. It gets your goal, experience, age range, today's check-in and note, and recent exercises, never your gender or bodyweight, and nothing is sent anywhere.

Other suggestions, all worked out on the phone

  • A focus for today when your week calls for one, such as "Try Legs".
  • A note that a lighter day may help when whole-body fatigue looks high.
  • An answer for your daily check-in from your Apple Health readings, if you turn Health on.
  • An offer to tune the recovery estimate to you, when how hard your sessions felt doesn't match it.
Privacy

Your training stays on your phone

There's no account and no server, and the developer can't see your data. Read the privacy policy.

  • No account, no server

    Nothing to sign up for. Workouts, profile and settings are stored on your iPhone, and nothing is sent to BodVod or anyone else.

  • Encrypted backups

    Save an encrypted backup with a recovery key you write down, or export your history as a readable file you own.

  • Face ID or Touch ID lock

    Optional. When it's on, BodVod asks when you open it and hides your log in the app switcher.

  • Online only for videos

    The only time BodVod goes online for content is when you tap play on an exercise video, which comes from YouTube.

Questions

When is BodVod coming out?

Soon, on the App Store for iPhone. This page will link to it once it's there.

How much does it cost?

BodVod will be free, with no subscription. There's an optional tip jar, and tips unlock nothing.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no account and no server, so there's nothing to sign up for and nothing to sync.

Which iPhones does it run on?

Any iPhone with iOS 17 or later. The on-device AI suggestions need an iPhone with Apple Intelligence turned on, on iOS 26 or later; without it, the workout engine plans everything as usual.

Can I train at home or outdoors?

Yes. Pick a home gym, park, beach or bodyweight only, or list your own equipment item by item, and change it for a single day when you train somewhere else.

Does it work with my watch?

If your watch or ring writes to Apple Health, BodVod can read sleep, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, workouts and steps from it once you turn Apple Health on. There's no Apple Watch app yet.

Is BodVod medical advice?

No. BodVod gives estimates and suggestions from what you log. It isn't medical advice, and training carries a risk of injury, so use your judgement and speak to a professional if you have a health condition.
