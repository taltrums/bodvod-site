BodVod Privacy Policy

Effective October 18, 2026

The short version

BodVod is a workout tracker made by Mohd Talha, an individual developer. It has no account and no server. Your workouts, profile, and settings stay on your phone, and nothing is sent to BodVod or to anyone else. The app has no analytics, advertising, or tracking of its own, and does not access contacts or location. The developer cannot see your data. The one time BodVod goes online for content is when you choose to play an exercise video, which comes from YouTube.

Data stored on your device

BodVod stores workout sessions, routines, exercise settings, check-ins, notes, profile answers, and app settings in the app's own storage on your phone. Profile answers include your age, gender, bodyweight, goal, experience, the equipment you have, body parts you trained recently, lifts and weights you already use, and any injuries or limits you add. They are used on the phone to choose exercises and set starting weights. iOS encrypts this storage while the phone is locked.

Apple Health

Apple Health is optional and off until you turn it on, either in Settings or from the one-time invitation BodVod shows. When it is on, BodVod reads sleep, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, steps, and workouts from Apple Health on your device, including what Apple Watch, Google Health (Fitbit and Pixel Watch), Garmin Connect, WHOOP, or other apps have shared there. BodVod uses these readings in memory to adjust the day's plan. It does not save them, include them in exports, send them anywhere, or write anything to Apple Health. It keeps only whether the setting is on, whether the invitation was shown, and the identifiers of workouts you have already answered about on the Today screen. You can remove BodVod's access at any time in the Health app under Sharing, then Apps.

Exercise videos from YouTube

Some form guides offer a video from YouTube. BodVod uses YouTube API Services to show it. Nothing is loaded from YouTube until you tap to play. When you do, the video is streamed from YouTube in its own player inside the app, and YouTube (Google) receives the information any embedded video sends, such as your IP address, device and browser details, and what you watch. While the video is open, YouTube may place or read cookies or similar technology, and may show content or ads from third parties. BodVod uses YouTube's privacy-enhanced player and clears those cookies when you close the video. Google handles this under its own privacy policy, not BodVod's: https://policies.google.com/privacy. BodVod doesn't send your workouts, profile, or health readings to YouTube, and doesn't receive anything back about you. If you don't play videos, YouTube is never contacted.

On-device suggestions

On iPhones with Apple Intelligence turned on, BodVod can ask Apple's on-device model to choose between a few exercises. It gives the model your goal, experience, age range, today's check-in and note, and recent exercises. Your gender and bodyweight are not given to it. This runs on the phone, and BodVod sends none of it anywhere.

Notifications

Rest timer alerts, and workout reminders if you turn them on, are scheduled on the phone. Nothing about them is sent anywhere.

Camera, photos, and sharing a workout

Sharing a workout is optional. The share card or video is made on the device and shows workout facts only: the exercises, sets, volume, time, and records, never your bodyweight, readiness, or notes. It goes only where you send it through the share sheet.

BodVod asks for the camera, and for the microphone when filming, only when you choose to take a photo or record a video for a share. Photos and videos you pick come through the system picker, so BodVod sees only the ones you choose, not your library. The camera, microphone, and photo library add permissions are used only for these steps. Pictures and videos are used to make the share and are not added to your workout history or backups. Working copies live in the app's temporary folder, protected like the workout log itself, and are removed when the next share begins or when you delete your data; a clip you film or pick is removed as soon as your numbers have been put on it.

Tips

The tip jar in Settings is optional. Opening it asks the App Store for the tips' prices, and leaving a tip is a normal App Store purchase made with your Apple Account; Apple's privacy policy applies to it. BodVod sees only whether the tip went through, and stores nothing about it. Once, after two weeks and a few saved workouts, BodVod offers the tip jar after a workout; it never asks again. That timing is worked out on your phone from the install date and your workout count, and is not sent anywhere.

Backups, exports, and files

BodVod does not sync your data to a BodVod cloud service. Your device's own backup settings may include app data in an iCloud or computer backup; that backup is controlled by your device settings and Apple, not by BodVod. Import and export happen only when you choose a file or destination through the system file picker or share sheet, and you choose where an export goes.

Retention and deletion

Your data stays on the device until you delete it or remove BodVod. Settings can delete every saved workout and routine; it asks first, and your settings stay. Deleting the app removes everything BodVod stored on the phone. Export a JSON archive or make an encrypted backup first if you want to keep a copy of your workouts; routines are not included in either. Data in a device backup may remain there according to your backup provider's settings.

Your rights

You can see, change, export, and delete your data in the app at any time. Because the developer does not receive any of it, there is no copy held elsewhere to ask for, correct, or erase.

European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and Switzerland. Your data is handled on your own device and under your control. BodVod reads Apple Health only after you turn it on and allow it, and you can withdraw that at any time in the Health app or in Settings. Playing a YouTube video is also your choice, and Google is responsible for what it receives when you do. You can contact the developer with questions, and you have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

United States. BodVod does not sell or share personal information, does not use it for targeted advertising or profiling, and does not collect, share, or sell consumer health data as defined by state laws such as Washington's My Health My Data Act or Nevada's consumer health data law. Health-related information you enter or allow BodVod to read stays on your phone.

Other countries. The same applies wherever you use BodVod: nothing is collected by the developer, and your data stays on your device. Playing a YouTube video works the same way everywhere, as described above.

Children

BodVod is not made for children under 13. It does not collect personal information from anyone. The age you give is used on the phone to choose suitable exercises.

Health information

BodVod suggests workouts and estimates recovery from what you log. It is not a medical device and is not medical advice. Training carries a risk of injury, so speak to a qualified professional if you have a health condition or are unsure whether an exercise suits you.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the app and at this policy's web address with a new effective date.

Contact
