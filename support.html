My workouts are gone after I deleted the app

Everything BodVod stores is kept on your phone only, so deleting the app removes it and the developer has no copy. Before you delete it or change phones, use Settings > App > Your data to export your workouts or make an encrypted backup.

How do I stop BodVod reading Apple Health?

Turn it off in BodVod's Settings > Profile > Apple Health, or remove access in the Health app under Sharing > Apps > BodVod.

How do I get a refund for a tip?

Tips are App Store purchases, so Apple handles refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com.
